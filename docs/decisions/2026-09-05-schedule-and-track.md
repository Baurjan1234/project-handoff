# Confirmed schedule, and we freeze on Sep 11 anyway

**Decision.** The dates below are confirmed from the ETHGlobal dashboard on 2026-09-05.
We keep the Sep 11 freeze and bank the two extra days as buffer rather than as scope.
The ETHGlobal track is **Building from Scratch**.

**The dates.** The dashboard states plainly that all times are shown in the viewer's
local timezone, which for this team is Asia/Ulaanbaatar. These are UB local.

| Event | When |
|---|---|
| Check-in 1 due | Tue Sep 8, 11:59 AM |
| Check-in 2 due | Fri Sep 11, 11:59 AM |
| Submissions due | Mon Sep 14, 12:00 AM |
| Judging round 1, asynchronous | Mon Sep 14, 03:00 AM |
| Judging round 2, live | Tue Sep 15, 12:00 AM |
| Finale | Thu Sep 17, 12:00 AM |

**Why this matters.** v1 guessed the deadline was Sep 12 or 13. It is Mon Sep 14 at
midnight, meaning end of Sunday the 13th, which is roughly two days more than assumed.
v1 also stated that judging is asynchronous and that the video is the submission. That is
true of round 1 only. **Round 2 is live**, and nobody was assigned to present at it.

**Why we still freeze on Sep 11.** Slack spent on scope is not slack. The two days are
for testnet flakiness, for the recording fallback, and for rehearsing a live round the
brief never planned for. Freezing early is the decision; the extra days are not a licence
to build more.

**Track.** ETHGlobal now splits prizes and judging criteria by track, and every team
member must be on the same track as the project in order to submit. Handoff is new, so
Building from Scratch is the only valid choice. The Continuity prize of $1,000 is not
available to us. **This is a click nobody has made yet and it blocks submission.**

**Consequences.** Someone owns the live round-2 presentation, which is a new duty not in
`team-seats.md`. Deadlines go on the Linear board, which is authoritative for dates.
